Today is the Sunday before the Nativity, and the Forefeast of Nativity is near. The Gospel reading is the opening of Matthew, which begins by tracing the ancestry of Jesus Christ. The Church draws our hearts toward Bethlehem.

We also remember the repose of St John of Kronstadt, Hieromartyr Ignatius the Godbearer, and our Righteous Father Philogonius, Bishop of Antioch.

Come, let us prepare room for Christ together. ☦️
