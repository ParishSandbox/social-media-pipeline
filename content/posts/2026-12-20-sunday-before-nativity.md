We are now just days away from the Nativity of Christ. On this Sunday before Nativity, the Church hears the Gospel from Matthew, chapter 1. We also keep the forefeast of the Nativity and remember the hieromartyr Ignatius the Godbearer, St Philogonius of Antioch, and the repose of St John of Kronstadt. As the fast draws toward its close, join us in prayer, and invite a neighbour to come and see. ☦️
