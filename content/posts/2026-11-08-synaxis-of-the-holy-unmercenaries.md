On this 23rd Sunday after Pentecost, the Church keeps the Synaxis of the Holy Unmercenaries, together with the Synaxis of Archangel Michael and the Bodiless Powers. We give thanks for the saints who cared for others in Christ's name, and for the heavenly hosts who glorify God without ceasing.

Come, pray with us, and bring a friend or neighbor to the Lord's house. All are welcome.

Holy Unmercenaries, pray to God for us! Holy Archangel Michael, pray for us! ☦️
