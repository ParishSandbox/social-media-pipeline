This Sunday the Church celebrates the Synaxis of the Holy Unmercenaries, the healer saints who cared for the sick without payment, together with the Synaxis of the Archangel Michael and the Bodiless Powers. We give thanks for the heavenly hosts who serve God and for every saint who showed Christ's compassion to others. Join us as we worship together this Sunday, the 23rd after Pentecost, and ask the holy angels and healers to pray for us, for our loved ones, and for all who suffer. ☦️
