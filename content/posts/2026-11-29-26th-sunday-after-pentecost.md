Today is the 26th Sunday after Pentecost, a day for the family of the Church to gather in worship. The Gospel is from Luke, chapter 13. As the Nativity Fast continues, we remember the holy martyr Paramon and his companions, the martyr Phaedrus, and our holy father Pitirim of Egypt. Their steadfast faith encourages us on the path toward the Nativity of Christ. We warmly invite our parishioners and neighbours to join us in prayer. ☦️
