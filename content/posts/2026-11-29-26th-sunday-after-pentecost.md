Every Sunday is a little Pascha, a weekly celebration of Christ's Resurrection. Today, the 26th Sunday after Pentecost, we gather to worship together as the Nativity Fast continues. The Gospel is from Luke, chapter 13, and the Epistle is from Ephesians, chapter 5.

We also remember the martyr Paramon and his companions, the martyr Phaedrus, and our Holy Father Pitirim of Egypt.

Neighbors and friends, you are always welcome among us. ☦️
