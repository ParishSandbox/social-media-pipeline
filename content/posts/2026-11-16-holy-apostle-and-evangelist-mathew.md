Today the Church celebrates the Holy Apostle and Evangelist Matthew. The Gospel appointed for the feast is from Matthew, chapter 9. We keep this feast during the Nativity Fast, a season in which we prepare our hearts to welcome Christ. Holy Apostle and Evangelist Matthew, pray to God for us! All are welcome to come and pray with our parish family, and to learn more about the Orthodox Christian faith. ☦️
