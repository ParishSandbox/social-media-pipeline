Today the Church celebrates the Holy Apostle and Evangelist Matthew, whose Gospel proclaims the good news of Jesus Christ. We give thanks for his witness and for the Scriptures that continue to guide and strengthen the faithful.

As we walk through the Nativity Fast, may his Gospel prepare our hearts to welcome the Lord at His birth.

Holy Apostle and Evangelist Matthew, pray to God for us! ☦️
