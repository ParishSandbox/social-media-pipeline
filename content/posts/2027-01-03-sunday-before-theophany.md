This Sunday is the Sunday before Theophany, and the Church begins the Forefeast, preparing our hearts to celebrate the manifestation of our Lord at His baptism. The appointed Gospel from Mark opens with the beginning of the Good News and the preaching of John the Baptist, who calls people to prepare the way.

We also remember the Prophet Malachi, the Holy Martyr Gordius of Caesarea, and our Holy Mother Genevieve of Paris, who stand as witnesses of faith across the centuries.

As Theophany draws near, we invite parishioners and neighbours to pray, to prepare, and to welcome the feast with joy. ☦️
