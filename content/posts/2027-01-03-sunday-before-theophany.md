Today is the Sunday before Theophany, and the Church is already turning toward the waters of the Jordan. In the days of the Forefeast we are called to prepare our hearts to meet Christ, who comes to be baptized.

The appointed Gospel from Mark begins with the preaching of John the Baptist, who prepares the way of the Lord. We also remember the Prophet Malachi, the Holy Martyr Gordius of Caesarea, and our Holy Mother Genevieve of Paris.

Come, pray with us, and let us make ready to welcome the feast of the Lord's manifestation. ☦️
