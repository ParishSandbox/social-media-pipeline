Today is the Sunday before Theophany, and the Church enters the forefeast of the Baptism of our Lord. As we prepare to celebrate Christ's appearing, the Gospel reading from Mark begins with the voice of the forerunner and the opening of the Good News. The Epistle from 2 Timothy invites us to keep the faith as we draw near to the feast.

We also remember the Prophet Malachi, the Holy Martyr Gordius of Caesarea, and our Holy Mother Genevieve of Paris. Today is a fast-free day, a gentle moment to gather in prayer and prepare our hearts for the blessing of the waters.

All are welcome to join us in worship, whether you are a longtime parishioner or a neighbour visiting for the first time. ☦️
