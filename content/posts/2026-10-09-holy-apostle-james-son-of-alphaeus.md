Today the Church commemorates the Holy Apostle James, Son of Alphaeus, one of those called by Christ to follow Him and proclaim the good news. We give thanks for the witness of the apostles, whose labors built up the Church we cherish today.

We also remember St Tikhon, Patriarch of Moscow and All Russia and Confessor, as well as Saints Andronicus and Athanasia, the righteous Abraham and Lot, and Hieromartyr Denis, first Bishop of Paris.

Holy saints of God, pray for us! ☦️
