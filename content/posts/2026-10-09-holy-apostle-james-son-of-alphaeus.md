Today we commemorate the Holy Apostle James, son of Alphaeus, one of the Lord's apostles. The Church also remembers St Tikhon, Patriarch of Moscow and All Russia and Confessor, Saints Andronicus and Athanasia, the righteous Abraham and Lot, St Stephen the Blind of Serbia, and the Hieromartyr Denis, first Bishop of Paris. What a great cloud of witnesses! May their prayers sustain us on our own path to the Kingdom, and may we take courage from their example. ☦️
