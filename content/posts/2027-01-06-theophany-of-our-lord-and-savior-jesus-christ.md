Today the Church celebrates the Theophany of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, a Great Feast of the Lord. At His baptism, Christ is revealed to the world, and we rejoice in the manifestation of God to His people.

The appointed Gospel reading from Matthew recounts the baptism of Jesus, and the Epistle to Titus speaks of the grace of God that has appeared for the salvation of all. This is a day of thanksgiving and renewal for the whole parish.

We warmly invite parishioners and neighbours to join in prayer and celebrate this great feast with us. ☦️
