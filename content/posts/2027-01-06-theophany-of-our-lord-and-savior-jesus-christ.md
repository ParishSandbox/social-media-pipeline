Today we celebrate the Theophany of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the great feast of His Baptism in the Jordan. The Gospel appointed for the feast, from Matthew, tells of Christ coming to be baptized by John, and the Church sings that the Holy Trinity is revealed to the world.

In His Baptism, Christ sanctifies the waters and calls all of creation to be renewed. It is a feast of light, of joy, and of new life for everyone who believes.

Blessed Theophany to our parish family and to all our neighbours! ☦️
