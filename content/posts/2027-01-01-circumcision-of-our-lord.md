Today the Church celebrates the Circumcision of Our Lord, a Great Feast, and remembers our father among the saints Basil the Great, along with the martyr Telemachos and St Gregory, Bishop of Nazianzus, father of St Gregory the Theologian. As we begin a new calendar year, we entrust our days, our families and our parish to the Lord. Wishing you a blessed new year, and welcoming all to pray with us. ☦️
