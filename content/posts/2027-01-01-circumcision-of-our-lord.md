As a new year begins, the Church keeps the feast of the Circumcision of Our Lord, still in the glow of the Nativity. We give thanks that God has truly come among us in the flesh.

Today we also remember our Father among the Saints Basil the Great, Telemachos the Martyr, and St Gregory, Bishop of Nazianzus.

May the Lord bless the year ahead with peace, health, and His abundant mercy. ☦️
