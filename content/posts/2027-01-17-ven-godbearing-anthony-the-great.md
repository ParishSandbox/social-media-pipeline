Today the Church commemorates our Venerable and Godbearing Father Anthony the Great, a beloved teacher of the monastic life whose example has guided Christians for centuries. We honour him with thanksgiving and ask for his prayers.

We also remember the pious Emperor Theodosius the Great and our Holy Father Makarios of Patmos.

May the memory of the saints encourage each of us to follow Christ more faithfully in the year ahead. All are welcome to join us in prayer this Sunday. 🕯️
