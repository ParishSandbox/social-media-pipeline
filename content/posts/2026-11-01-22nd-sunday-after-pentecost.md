On this 22nd Sunday after Pentecost, we gather to worship the risen Christ. The Church also remembers the holy unmercenaries and wonderworkers Cosmas and Damian, along with their mother, St Theodota, and the holy new martyr Helen of Sinope. The Gospel appointed for the day is from Luke, chapter 8. May the prayers of the holy healers who served freely, without payment, inspire us to serve others with generous and loving hearts. All are welcome. ☦️
