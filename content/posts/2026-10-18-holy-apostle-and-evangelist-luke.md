On this 20th Sunday after Pentecost, the Church also celebrates the Holy Apostle and Evangelist Luke. We remember as well the holy martyr Marinos. Every Sunday we gather as one family around the Lord's table, giving thanks for the Gospel handed down to us. The Gospel reading today is from Luke, chapter 8. Whether you are a lifelong parishioner or a curious neighbour, you are warmly welcome to worship with us. May Saint Luke pray for us all. ☦️
