On this 20th Sunday after Pentecost we gather to worship the risen Lord, and we also celebrate the feast of the Holy Apostle and Evangelist Luke. Through his Gospel, the Church hears the good news of Christ, and today's Gospel reading comes from Luke's own account.

We also remember the holy martyr Marinos, who bore witness to Christ.

Holy Apostle and Evangelist Luke, pray to God for us! ☦️
