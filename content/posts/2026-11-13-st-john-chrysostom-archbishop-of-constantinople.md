Today the Church celebrates our Father among the Saints John Chrysostom, Archbishop of Constantinople. We give thanks to God for his ministry as a shepherd and teacher of the Church, and ask for his prayers as we seek to hear and live the Gospel.

May the Lord grant us hearts that are attentive to His word, today and every day.

Holy Father John Chrysostom, pray to God for us! ☦️
