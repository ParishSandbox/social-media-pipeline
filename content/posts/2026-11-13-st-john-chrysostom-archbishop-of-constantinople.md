Today the Church honors St John Chrysostom, Archbishop of Constantinople, a beloved Father and teacher of the Church. The Gospel reading for the day is from Luke, chapter 13. As we walk through these days, his example invites us to listen closely to the Gospel and put it into practice in our daily lives. Holy Father John, pray to God for us, for our parish, and for all who seek to follow Christ more faithfully. ☦️
