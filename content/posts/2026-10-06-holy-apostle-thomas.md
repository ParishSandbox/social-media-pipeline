Today the Church honors the Holy Apostle Thomas, one of the chosen followers of Christ who was sent out to bear witness to the Gospel. His life invites each of us to know the Lord more deeply and to trust Him with our whole heart.

We also remember the repose of St Innocent, Enlightener of Alaska and Siberia and Metropolitan of Moscow, who carried the Orthodox faith to distant lands.

Holy Apostle Thomas and St Innocent, pray to God for us! ☦️
