Today the Church honors the Holy Apostle Thomas, one of the Lord's chosen apostles. We also remember the repose of St Innocent, Enlightener of Alaska and Siberia and Metropolitan of Moscow, who carried the Gospel to far-off peoples. May the holy apostles and the saints of every land strengthen our faith and teach us to follow Christ with courage and humility. Holy Apostle Thomas, pray to God for us! All are welcome to pray with our parish family, whether you are a lifelong parishioner or a curious neighbour. ☦️
