Every Sunday is a little Pascha, a weekly celebration of Christ's Resurrection. Today, the 21st Sunday after Pentecost, we gather as the Lord's people to pray, to hear the Scriptures, and to be nourished together. Today's Gospel is from Luke, chapter 16, and the Epistle is from Galatians, chapter 2.

We also remember the holy martyrs Marcian and Martyrius the Notaries, and St Tabitha of Joppa.

Neighbors and friends, you are always welcome among us. ☦️
