Each Sunday is a little Pascha, a day when the Church gathers to worship the risen Lord. Today, the 21st Sunday after Pentecost, the Gospel appointed is from Luke, chapter 16, the account of the rich man and Lazarus, a reminder to open our hearts to those in need around us. We also remember the holy martyrs Marcian and Martyrius the Notaries and St Tabitha of Joppa. Come, join our parish family in prayer, and bring a neighbour with you. ☦️
