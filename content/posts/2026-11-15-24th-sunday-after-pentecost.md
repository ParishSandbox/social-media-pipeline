Today, the 24th Sunday after Pentecost, the Nativity Fast begins, a season of prayer, almsgiving, and preparation for the coming of Christ at Bethlehem. The Gospel appointed is from Luke, chapter 10, the parable of the Good Samaritan, a call to show mercy to our neighbour. We also remember the martyrs Guria, Shamuna and Habib of Edessa and our venerable father Paisius Velichkovsky. May this season draw us closer to God and to one another. Come and pray with us. ☦️
