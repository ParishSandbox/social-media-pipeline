Today is the 24th Sunday after Pentecost, and it is also the beginning of the Nativity Fast. As we prepare to welcome Christ's birth, the Church invites us to prayer, simplicity, and generosity toward others.

We also remember Guria, Shamuna, and Habib, Martyrs and Confessors of Edessa, and our Venerable Father Paisius Velichkovsky.

Whether you are familiar with the fast or curious about it, we would love to walk this road together. ☦️
