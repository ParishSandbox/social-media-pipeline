On this Sunday after Theophany, the Church continues to rejoice in the manifestation of our Lord at the Jordan. The light revealed at the feast does not fade after one day; we are invited to carry it into the new year and into our daily lives.

Today we also remember St Gregory of Nyssa, Dometian, Bishop of Melitene, and St Theophan the Recluse. The appointed Gospel, from Matthew, tells of Christ beginning His ministry.

Come and pray with us, and let us keep the joy of Theophany alive in our homes. ☦️
