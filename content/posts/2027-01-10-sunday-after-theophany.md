This Sunday after Theophany, we continue to rejoice in the manifestation of our Lord. The Gospel reading from Matthew tells of Jesus beginning His ministry, and the Epistle to the Ephesians speaks of the gifts Christ gives to His Church.

Today we also remember St Gregory of Nyssa, Dometian, Bishop of Melitene, and St Theophan the Recluse, faithful shepherds and teachers whose lives point us toward Christ.

Parishioners and neighbours are warmly welcome to pray with us as we carry the light of Theophany into the new year. ☦️
