Christ is born! Glorify Him! We continue to celebrate the Nativity of the Lord on this Sunday after Nativity. The Church also remembers the holy Protomartyr Stephen, along with St Theodore, Patriarch of Constantinople, and St Theodore the Branded. The Gospel is from Matthew, chapter 2. May the joy of the feast fill our homes and our parish. All are welcome to worship with us. ☦️
