Christ is born! Glorify Him!

On this Sunday after Nativity, the Church continues to rejoice in the birth of our Lord, and also remembers the Protomartyr Stephen. The Gospel reading is from Matthew, chapter 2, and the Epistle is from Galatians, chapter 1.

We also remember Theodore, Patriarch of Constantinople, and our Righteous Father Theodore the Branded.

May the joy of the feast fill your home. ☦️
