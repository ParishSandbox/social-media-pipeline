Today the Church keeps the great feast of the Entry of the Most-Holy Theotokos into the Temple. We celebrate the Virgin Mary, who was set apart for God and who would become the Mother of our Lord.

This feast arrives during the Nativity Fast, turning our hearts toward the coming birth of Christ and reminding us to offer our own lives to God.

Most Holy Theotokos, save us! ☦️
