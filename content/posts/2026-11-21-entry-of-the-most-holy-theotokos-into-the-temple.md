Today we celebrate the Entry of the Most-Holy Theotokos into the Temple, a great feast of the Mother of God. The feast falls within the Nativity Fast and turns our hearts toward Bethlehem, as we honor the Virgin Mary, whom the Church loves and venerates above all the saints. Most Holy Theotokos, save us! Join us in prayer as we celebrate this feast, and invite a friend or neighbour to come with you. ☦️
