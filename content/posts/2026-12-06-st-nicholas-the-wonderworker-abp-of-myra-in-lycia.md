This 27th Sunday after Pentecost, the Church also celebrates St Nicholas the Wonderworker, Archbishop of Myra in Lycia, one of the most beloved saints of the Orthodox Church. We give thanks to God for his holy life and ask for his prayers during the Nativity Fast.

We also remember the New Martyr Nicolas Karamos of Smyrna and St Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and Vladimir.

Holy Father Nicholas, pray to God for us! ☦️
