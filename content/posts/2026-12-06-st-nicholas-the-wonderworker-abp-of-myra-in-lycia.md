This Sunday we celebrate St Nicholas the Wonderworker, Archbishop of Myra in Lycia, one of the most beloved saints of the Church. His feast falls on the 27th Sunday after Pentecost, in the midst of the Nativity Fast. We also remember the new martyr Nicolas Karamos of Smyrna and St Maximus, Metropolitan of Kiev and Vladimir. Holy Father Nicholas, pray to God for us! Come, celebrate with our parish family and bring your friends and neighbours. ☦️
