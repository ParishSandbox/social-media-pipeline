Today is the Sunday of the Forefathers, when the Church remembers the righteous people who looked ahead to Christ's coming, as we draw nearer to the Nativity. Today's Gospel is from Luke, chapter 14.

We also remember the repose of Venerable Herman of Alaska, Wonderworker of All America, Hieromartyr Juvenaly, Protomartyr of America, and the holy virgin martyr Lucy of Syracuse.

Join us as we continue to prepare for the birth of our Lord. ☦️
