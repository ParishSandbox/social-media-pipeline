Today the Church keeps the Sunday of the Forefathers, as we draw nearer to the Nativity of Christ. The Gospel is from Luke, chapter 14. We also remember the repose of St Herman of Alaska, Wonderworker of All America, the hieromartyr Juvenaly, Protomartyr of America, and the holy virgin martyr Lucy of Syracuse. May the prayers of all the saints prepare our hearts to receive Christ at His Nativity. All are welcome to worship with us. ☦️
