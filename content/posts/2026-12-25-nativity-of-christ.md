Christ is born! Glorify Him!

Today the Church celebrates the Nativity of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the great feast on which God comes among us. We also remember the shepherds who saw the Lord and the veneration of the Magi, who came to worship Him.

From our parish family to yours, we wish you a blessed and joyful feast. Whoever you are, you are welcome to celebrate with us. ☦️
