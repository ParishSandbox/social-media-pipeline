Christ is born! Glorify Him! Today the Church celebrates the Nativity of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, a Great Feast of the Lord. We remember the shepherds who saw the Lord and the veneration of the Magi. The Gospel is from Matthew, chapter 2. Our parish family wishes you and your loved ones a blessed feast, and we warmly welcome our neighbours to celebrate with us. ☦️
