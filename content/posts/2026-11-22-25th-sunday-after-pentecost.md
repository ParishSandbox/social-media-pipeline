Every Sunday is a little Pascha, a weekly celebration of Christ's Resurrection. Today, the 25th Sunday after Pentecost, we gather in prayer during the Nativity Fast. The Gospel reading is from Luke, chapter 12, and the Epistle is from Ephesians, chapter 4.

We also remember the Apostles of the Seventy Philemon, Archippus, Apphia, and Onesimus, St Michael, Prince of Tver, and the holy martyrs Cecilia, Valerian, Tiburtius and Maximus.

You are welcome to join us. ☦️
