On this 25th Sunday after Pentecost, the Church gathers to worship Christ and to hear the Gospel from Luke, chapter 12. During the Nativity Fast we are invited to keep our hearts focused on what truly matters. We also remember the apostles of the Seventy Philemon, Archippus, Apphia and Onesimus, as well as St Michael, Prince of Tver, and the holy martyrs Cecilia, Valerian, Tiburtius and Maximus of Rome. May their prayers keep us steadfast. All are welcome. ☦️
